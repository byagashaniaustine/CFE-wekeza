## Full description

Most Tanzanians don't lack the will to invest — they lack a trusted, jargon-free guide and a clear first step. CFE.Wekeza meets people on WhatsApp, the app they already open every day, and teaches them how money grows: saving versus investing, emergency funds, unit trusts, shares, government securities, and pensions. Every lesson is grounded in the **Curriculum for Certified Financial Educators (CFE)** issued by Tanzania's National Council for Financial Inclusion (2023), and delivered in both **Kiswahili and English** through a tap-through menu of interactive buttons and lists — no app to download, no forms to fill just to learn.

Beyond lessons, the bot helps people *act*. An AI tutor answers free-text questions in either language, so nobody gets stuck. Interactive quizzes check understanding with plain-language explanations. A growth simulation and the **DSE Scholar Challenge** make investing tangible instead of abstract. And when someone is ready, a guided onboarding flow hands them off to real, **CMSA- and BoT-regulated** channels — UTT AMIS unit trusts, DSE brokers, and Bank of Tanzania securities — with the exact steps, minimums, and fees.

Crucially, it also teaches people to protect themselves. Alongside the "how to invest" content sits clear scam-awareness guidance — spotting "guaranteed 30% a month" schemes, pyramid recruitment, and unlicensed "forex experts," and checking licences at CMSA — because financial inclusion without fraud awareness is a trap.

## Key features

- **Bilingual by design** — Kiswahili and English, with automatic language detection.
- **Curriculum-grade content** — built on the CFE (modules CFE01–CFE05): saving vs. investing, UTT unit trusts, DSE shares, government securities, pensions/NSSF, and scam protection.
- **Structured learning paths** — Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced investor tracks, plus product "academies" for UTT AMIS, DSE, Government Securities, and Pensions.
- **AI tutor** — answers open-ended questions in either language (Claude, with an automatic Gemini fallback), so no question goes unanswered.
- **Quizzes with explanations** — short assessments that reinforce each topic.
- **Make it tangible** — a growth simulation and the DSE Scholar Challenge show what investing over time actually looks like.
- **Guided onboarding** — routes ready users to regulated platforms with real steps, minimums, and fees; optional lead capture for partners.
- **No app required** — runs entirely on WhatsApp (buttons, lists, and Flows); progress, language, and quiz scores are remembered for 30 days.

## Who it's for

Everyday Tanzanians — students, salaried workers, and informal-sector earners — who want to grow their money but find banks and brokerages intimidating. And the institutions behind them — fund managers, regulators, and financial educators — who need to reach people at scale, in their own language, on the channel they already use.

## Trust & compliance

CFE.Wekeza delivers **education, not financial advice**. It promotes only CMSA- and BoT-regulated channels, cites its sources (UTT AMIS, DSE, BoT, CMSA), and closes every topic with a clear disclaimer. Figures such as minimums, fees, and returns are researched against primary sources and meant to be re-verified periodically.
